# Extended Laptop Protection Plan

Product: 14-inch ultrabook laptop
Purchase date: April 3, 2026

## Coverage

The plan covers accidental damage, including drops, spills and cracked screens, as well as hardware failures. Coverage lasts 3 years from the purchase date.

## Claims

Claims are filed by phone or through the online claims center. Each accidental damage claim has a $99 deductible. Hardware failure claims have no deductible. A maximum of 2 accidental damage claims are covered per year.

## Exclusions

Loss, theft, cosmetic damage that does not affect function, and damage from unauthorized repairs are not covered.
