# Residential Lease Agreement

This lease is made between Harbor View Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Jordan Reyes ("Tenant") for Unit 4B, 1420 Alder Street, Riverside.

## Term

The lease begins on January 1, 2026 and ends on December 31, 2026. After the end date the lease converts to month-to-month unless either party gives notice.

## Rent and Fees

Monthly rent is $1,450, due on the 1st of each month. Rent received after the 5th of the month incurs a late fee of $75. Rent may be paid by bank transfer or through the tenant portal.

The security deposit is $2,900, refundable within 21 days of move-out less any deductions for damage beyond normal wear and tear.

## Pets

One cat is permitted with a one-time pet deposit of $300. Dogs are not permitted without written approval from the Landlord.

## Moving Out

Tenant must give at least 60 days written notice before moving out. Notice must be delivered by email to the property manager or by certified mail.

## Maintenance

Tenant is responsible for replacing light bulbs and smoke detector batteries. All other repairs must be reported through the tenant portal. Emergency maintenance is available 24 hours a day.
