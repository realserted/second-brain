# Summit Fitness Membership Agreement

Member: Jordan Reyes
Plan: All-Access

## Fees

The monthly membership fee is $39. An annual maintenance fee of $49 is charged every June.

## Benefits

The All-Access plan includes all locations, group classes, and 2 guest passes per month. Personal training sessions are billed separately.

## Cancellation

To cancel, the member must submit a cancellation request in writing at least 30 days before the next billing date. Memberships can be frozen for up to 3 months per year for a $10 monthly freeze fee.
