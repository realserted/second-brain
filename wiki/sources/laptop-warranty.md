# Extended Laptop Protection Plan

Protection plan for a 14-inch ultrabook laptop purchased April 3, 2026 (warranties/laptop-warranty.md).

## Key facts
- Covers accidental damage (drops, spills, cracked screens) and hardware failures for 3 years from purchase (warranties/laptop-warranty.md)
- Accidental damage claims: $99 deductible each, at most 2 per year (warranties/laptop-warranty.md)
- Hardware failure claims: no deductible (warranties/laptop-warranty.md)
- Claims by phone or the online claims center (warranties/laptop-warranty.md)
- Not covered: loss, theft, cosmetic damage that does not affect function, damage from unauthorized repairs (warranties/laptop-warranty.md)
