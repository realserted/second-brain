# Notice periods and getting out of contracts

| Contract | Notice or exit cost | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Lease | 60 days written notice, by email to the property manager or by certified mail | [[apartment-lease]] (leases/apartment-lease.md) |
| Freelance MSA | 14 days written notice, either party | [[freelance-msa]] (contracts/freelance-msa.md) |
| Gym | Written request at least 30 days before the next billing date | [[gym]] (memberships/gym.md) |
| Internet | No notice period stated; leaving before February 15, 2027 costs a $150 early termination fee, prorated | [[internet-service]] (contracts/internet-service.md) |

Moving house touches the lease, the internet contract and the [[1420-alder-street]] address on other documents.
