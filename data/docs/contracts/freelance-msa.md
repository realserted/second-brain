# Master Services Agreement

This agreement is between Jordan Reyes ("Contractor") and Acme Analytics Inc ("Client"), effective March 1, 2026.

## Services and Rate

Contractor provides data engineering and dashboard development services. The hourly rate is $85 per hour, billed in 15-minute increments.

## Invoicing and Payment

Contractor invoices monthly on the last business day of the month. Payment terms are Net 30 from the invoice date. Late payments accrue interest at 1% per month.

## Intellectual Property

All work product is assigned to the Client upon full payment of the related invoice.

## Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement with 14 days written notice. Client must pay for all work performed up to the termination date.

## Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential information private for 2 years after the agreement ends.
